About

I first started taking photographs in fifth grade, using my sister’s old iPhone 5 during trips to Jiuzhaigou and the UK. What began as a way to save beautiful scenes gradually became a habit of paying attention—to light on a wall, a changing sky, and the small, passing details of everyday life.

In high school, a photography class gave me my first systematic introduction to the medium. My Canon EOS 750D travelled with me for years; today, I mainly use a Fujifilm X-S20 and an Olympus Zoom-105 film camera. I photograph nature, cities, film, and travel, with a soft spot for what feels unplanned and alive.

Yunnan is one of my favourite places to return to. I like travelling slowly and staying long enough to understand a place’s rhythm. One of my clearest memories is sitting on the rooftop of a guesthouse in Dali, with the Cangshan Mountains behind me and Erhai Lake in front—watching the light move across the water in the morning and again at dusk.

This is a place for photographs, but also for travel notes, books, films, music, questions, and ordinary things worth keeping. Outside of photography and travel, I enjoy reading, cinema, music, and working out. I hope it feels like an ongoing record: thoughtful at times, everyday at others, and always personal.

我最早开始拍照，是在小学五年级。那时我用姐姐淘汰下来的 iPhone 5，在九寨沟和英国旅行时随手记录风景、街道和身边的人。后来，摄影慢慢让我更认真地观察生活：一束光落在墙上、一个陌生城市傍晚的颜色，以及那些不想忘记的平凡瞬间。

高中时，学校的摄影课让我第一次系统地学习摄影。佳能 EOS 750D 是我的第一台相机；现在我主要使用 Fujifilm X-S20 和 Olympus Zoom-105 胶片相机，拍摄自然、城市、胶片和旅行。我尤其喜欢照片里不那么刻意的部分——天气、光线、人群和时间留下的痕迹。

云南是我最喜欢的地方之一。我喜欢慢慢旅行，也喜欢在一个地方多停留几天，感受它自己的节奏。印象最深的是一次住在大理的民宿：屋顶背靠苍山，面前就是洱海。早晨和傍晚，我会爬上去坐一会儿，看光线一点点掠过湖面和天空。

这里会放照片，也会留下旅行、书籍、电影、音乐、思考，以及日常里值得记住的小事。摄影和旅行之外，我也喜欢阅读、电影、音乐和健身。希望它像一份持续更新的个人记录：有时认真思考，有时只是好好生活，但始终是属于我的东西。
